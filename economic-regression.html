What Makes an Economic Regression Useful?

Starter article — replace this with your own writing.

Start with the question

A useful regression begins with a clearly stated economic question. The variables and model should follow the question rather than the other way around.

Choose an empirical framework

Once the question is clear, define the outcome, the main explanatory variable and the controls needed to represent the economic mechanism being studied.

Interpret carefully

Regression output is evidence within a model, not automatically proof of causality. Coefficients should be interpreted alongside the assumptions, data quality and identification strategy.

Good empirical work connects economic reasoning, measurement and transparent statistical evidence.
