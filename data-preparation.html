From Raw Data to an Analysis-Ready Dataset

Starter article — replace this with your own writing.

Inspect before transforming

Start by understanding the variables, missing values, coding conventions and units. A quick data dictionary can prevent mistakes later.

Clean and recode

Standardise variable names, handle missing values deliberately and document any recoding decisions. Keep the raw data separate from the analysis-ready version.

Validate

Check ranges, duplicates, summary statistics and relationships that should hold economically or logically before fitting a model.
