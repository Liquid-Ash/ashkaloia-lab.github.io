Research project · Aug–Sep 2025

Macroeconomic Empirical Analysis

An empirical analysis of macroeconomic variables with a focus on cyclical components.

Overview

Under the guidance of Asst. Prof. Sargam Gupta at IGIDR, the project decomposed variables such as GDP, total consumption and net exports using real and seasonally adjusted values.

Approach

The analysis focused on computing standard-deviation ratios of cyclical components and using these measures to examine key macroeconomic relationships.

Result

The work was designed to verify established macroeconomic results through empirical decomposition and comparison of cyclical behaviour.
