Econometrics · Aug–Sep 2025

Economic Growth Shocks & Civil Conflict

An empirical study of the relationship between economic growth shocks and civil conflict probability in Sub-Saharan African countries.

Research question

How do economic growth shocks relate to the probability of civil conflict?

Methodology

The project used OLS regression models to analyse the effect of economic growth shocks on civil conflict probability.

Scope

The analysis focused on countries in Sub-Saharan Africa and used an empirical regression framework to study the relationship.
